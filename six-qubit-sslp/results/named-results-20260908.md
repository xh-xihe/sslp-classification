## What a row means

**covered** means a reviewed pairing: a reader proposed the declarations
that state or prove the result, and an adversarial checker read those
declarations and tried to refute the pairing. The rule applied throughout
is that a checked declaration has to carry the *whole* statement; a
declaration proving a special case, or one direction of an equivalence, is
not enough. It is a review judgement, not a mechanical proof that the LaTeX
and the Lean say the same thing.

**covered does not mean the manuscript's own proof was formalized step by
step.** Several orbit no-gos here take a deliberately repaired route --
orbit 26 closes a mixed-axis branch the manuscript passes over, orbit 30
replaces a two-point magnitude argument with a support-graph one. The
*statement* is proved; a particular intermediate line of the manuscript's
own argument may have no counterpart, and those displays are recorded
`partial` in the ledger with a reason.

Line numbers are into the manuscript sources, which are not redistributed
here. No manuscript text appears in this file: the group headings below are
written from the label names, and the prose under each entry is our review.
