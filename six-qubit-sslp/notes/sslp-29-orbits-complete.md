## The four that were hard, and what closed them

* **33** -- a `2x2` block reflection on the zero-angle qubit.  `C^{-1} J C =
  2 R 1^T - I` turns the diagonal conditions into
  `C_i C_i^dagger + D_i D_i^dagger = 2 R_i rho`; see
  [[sslp-orbit33-block-recipe]].
* **29** -- pair coordinates in `C^2` and a `K_4` of orthogonalities; the trick
  that shortened it was never picking a nonzero vector but CONSTRUCTING the
  direction `u := (-star v_1, star v_0)`; see [[sslp-orbit29-pair-coords]].
* **31** -- the `4x4` table.  Family one says the four bottom columns are
  dependent with coefficients `v_e = conj(d at the complement of A_e)`, so
  `sum_e v_e M_{ef} = 0` for each `f`; family three pins
  `M_{ef} = conj(v_e) v_f` off the diagonal.  Any column alone in its phase class
  dies at once.  See [[sslp-orbit31-structure]], [[sslp-orbit31-indexmap]].
* **26** -- the CROSS-ANCHOR contraction (contract the outer identity with `D_7`
  on one index and `conj(C_6)` on the other) closes the mixed-axis branch that
  an earlier argument silently assumed away; see [[sslp-orbit26-hexagon]].

## What actually made the difference

A tooling bug: `scan.dressings` under-generated the admissible Pauli set,
dropping every `Y_i Z_j` and all pure-`Z` conditions.  The correct count is
`154 = 1 + 6*3 + 15*9`.  Three of the last four orbits were blocked by it and
none of the earlier certifications was affected (they used a SUBSET of the true
conditions).  See [[kl-dressings-undercount]].

Five reasoning errors were made and corrected along the way, all the same shape:
asserting a step without checking it against the originals.  The detection
methods, in increasing order of usefulness -- rref against the mask row space;
replaying a reduced system's solution against the RAW Knill-Laflamme conditions;
an adversarial workflow that produced explicit counterexamples; and, best of all,
WRITING THE LEAN, which forces a vague step to become concrete and often
surfaces the counterexample itself.  Do not derive at length on paper and
formalise in one batch; formalise as you go.

For the final two orbits the work was delegated to workflows with three
non-negotiable instructions: verify by machine rather than assert, actually
compile rather than report compiling, and classify every hypothesis of the
headline theorem as a genuine Knill-Laflamme condition, the `MomentSystem`, or
something else -- anything in the third class is a smuggled assumption and turns
the proof into a shell.  Orbit 31's headline has 46 hypotheses (33 `offSum = 0`,
12 `diagBot = diagTop`, 1 `MomentSystem`) and orbit 26's has 41 (24 / 16 / 1);
both were classified by hand after the fact, with zero in the third class.
