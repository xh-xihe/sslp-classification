Orbit 25 (`a = (0,1,1,1,1,1)`, `q = 4`, `delta = 1`, fibers 12+12) is the only
orbit that needs the DIAGONAL Knill-Laflamme conditions. Its phase ratios are
globally consistent but all twenty-five cross relations carry sign `+1`, so no
odd cycle exists. Every step below is verified numerically to machine precision.

Indexing: `S0 = [000000, 001111, 010111, 011011, 011101, 011110, 100000, 101111,
110111, 111011, 111101, 111110]`, `hat` is the reversal `hat k = 11 - k`, and the
bottom fiber is closed under flipping qubit one, with `S0[k+6] = S0[k] xor 100000`.
Write `u = star . c`, `v = d`, `D_k = star(c_k) * c_{k+6}`, `A = D_0`,
`E_k = star(d_k) * d_{k+6}`.

1. The quadratic closure pins every probability: bottom `3/16, 1/16 x5, 3/16,
   1/16 x5`; top `1/16 x5, 3/16, 1/16 x5, 3/16`. (Matches the paper.)
2. Off-diagonal relations give `c_0 c_{6+k} = c_k c_6`. Multiplying by
   `c_0 c_k` and using `star z * z = normSq z` yields `q_0 D_k = q_k A`, hence
   `3 D_k = A` for `k = 1..5`.
3. The phase-ratio links radiate from TWO hubs, 0 and 6, each joined to the other
   ten points but NOT to each other; `0 ~ 6` needs one transitivity step (through
   9, say). From hub 0 they read `u_0 v_{hat a} = eps_a u_a v_{hat 0}` with
   `eps_0 = +1`, `eps_1..eps_5 = -1`, `eps_6 = -1` (transitive),
   `eps_7..eps_11 = +1`.
4. Multiplying two of those and using `star(u_0) u_0 = q_0` gives, division-free,
       `q_0 E_k = eps_{11-k} eps_{5-k} * r_{hat 0} * D_{5-k}`.
   The sign product is `-1` for every k, and `q_0 = r_{hat 0} = 3/16`, so
   `E_k = -D_{5-k}` uniformly.
5. The mask `X_1 = 100000` has twelve diagonal incidences on each fiber. Its two
   surviving dressings (`z = 000000` and `z = 100000`) give
   `sum_k (D_k + star D_k) = sum_k (E_k + star E_k)` and
   `sum_k (star D_k - D_k) = sum_k (star E_k - E_k)`; adding and subtracting,
   `sum_k D_k = sum_k E_k`.
6. By step 4 that right side is `- sum_k D_k`, so `sum_k D_k = 0`.
7. By step 2, `sum_k D_k = A + 5A/3 = (8/3) A`, so `A = 0` -- against
   `normSq A = q_0 q_6 = 9/256`.

This is shorter than the paper's route, which introduces `K_0 = A - (5/3) S` and
`K_1 = S - (5/3) A` and compares them; here it is the two halves of a single
diagonal condition.

The Lean diagonal layer (`diagBot`, `diagTop`, and all incidence/flip/sign
lemmas for `X_1`) is generated by `tools/generators/gen15.py` and compiles. What
remains is wiring steps 2-7.
